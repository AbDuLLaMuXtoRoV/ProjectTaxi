    Questions

    Answers to common questions

    How will I find my driver?

    The driver waits for you in the arrivals hall, right at the exit, holding a sign with your name. One hour before the pickup we send you the driver's name, phone number and car number.

    What if my flight is delayed?

    Nothing to worry about. We follow your flight and the driver arrives when you actually land. After landing you have 60 minutes of free waiting time.

    How can I pay?

    After the ride, directly to the driver: cash in soʻm, a bank card (Uzcard, Humo, Visa, Mastercard) or via Payme, Click or Uzum. No prepayment is needed.

    Can I book a ride for my parents or a guest?

    Yes. Choose «Someone else» and enter the passenger's name and phone. The driver will call them, and you will also receive all trip updates.

    Do you have child seats?

    Yes, free of charge. Just say how many you need when booking.

    How do I cancel or change a booking?

    Cancellation is free up to 3 hours before pickup. Use «My booking» at the top of the page, or simply call us.

    I don't have a local SIM card or internet. What do I do?

    Don't worry — the driver will find you by the name sign in the arrivals hall. The airport also has free Wi-Fi, and you can always call our 24/7 number.

    Is the price really final?

    Yes. The price you see includes waiting time, flight tracking, luggage and child seats. No extra charges at night, on weekends or on holidays.

    Prefer to talk to a person?

    Our operators answer 24/7 in Uzbek, Russian and English and will book the ride for you.
