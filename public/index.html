    Answers to common questions

    Where will I meet my chauffeur?

    Your chauffeur waits in the baggage claim area holding a sign with your name.

    What if my flight is delayed?

    Nothing to worry about. We track your flight and the chauffeur adjusts to the actual landing time.

    What is included in the price?

    Everything: tolls, taxes, airport fees, parking and the chauffeur's gratuity.

    How do I pay?

    After the ride, with any major credit or debit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or cash.

    Can I book a ride for my parents or a guest?

    Yes. Choose “Someone else” and enter the passenger's name and phone.

    Do you provide child seats?

    Yes — infant, toddler and booster seats are free.

    How do I cancel or change a booking?

    Cancellation is free up to 3 hours before pickup.

    Why not just take a rideshare?

    We meet you inside with a name sign, track your flight and never apply surge pricing.

    Prefer to talk to a person?

    Our dispatchers answer 24/7 in English and Spanish and will book the ride for you.
